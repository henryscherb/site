I am an ambitious but disciplined self-starter with a battle-tested BD, strategy, and operations toolkit, and a personally rooted passion to serve unmet health markets.

I leverage my MS from Tufts School of Engineering to design, manage, and deliver consulting projects and advise biopharma executives on commercialization & corporate strategy. Family experience with spinal cord injury led me to co-found BreakThru SCI, where I run strategy, operations, partnerships, finance and fundraising for a national patient advocacy organization that partners with biotechs and brings their patient voice to the FDA and Congress. Before that, I co-founded Amoji, a neurotrauma health tech startup, and rose from intern to Director of Strategy & BD at a disability-tech VC.
