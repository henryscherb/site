02 · Regulatory & Clinical Advocacy
BreakThru SCI · Co-founder, Chief Strategy & Operations Officer · Aug 2025–present

Getting restorative spinal cord injury therapies to patients faster, by bringing the patient voice to Congress, the FDA and the companies building them.

I co-founded BreakThru SCI, a national patient advocacy organization working to speed restorative SCI treatments to patients. We also push for regulatory change, so SCI trials can use designs that fit how the condition actually varies from patient to patient. I lead the organization's strategy and operations, own its partnerships, run fundraising, and built and manage its board.

Why I started BreakThru

When my brother suffered a spinal cord injury, I pledged my life to improving his. In his clinical trial, he and others in his cohort saw real, meaningful changes, but the trial didn't measure them, so they weren't taken seriously. We started advocating right away for regulators to account for the meaningful and variable outcomes SCI trials produce, and that became BreakThru SCI.
