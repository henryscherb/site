LIKE Ventures

Disability-tech venture capital · Director of Strategy & Business Development (part-time, during Tufts) · previously Intern · 2021–2024
How I got in

After James Gillespie, PhD, JD, MPA, gave a talk at the company where I was interning, I caught him in the elevator and told him I wanted to work for him. After vetting and interviews, he offered me an internship at LIKE Ventures.

Director of Strategy and Business Development

  • Board and leadership alignment: facilitated alignment between the board, senior leadership and the executive team.
  • Strategic partnerships: sourced and established partnerships that expanded the firm's reach and fundraising opportunities.
  • Built the deal-sourcing system: pitched, built and ran a system for deal sourcing and inbound pitches that cut the diligence workload.
  • Portfolio support: advised 5 portfolio companies (adaptive clothing, smart prosthetics, electrical stimulation rehab, a robotic arm device and adaptive vehicles) on commercialization, fundraising and deal negotiation, and worked with the Verizon Startup Accelerator on incubation.
  • Flagship conference: led and moderated two annual accessibility technology conferences for 500+ investors, LPs, founders, patients and researchers.

Intern (first 1.5 years)

  • Researched and diligenced disability-tech startups.
  • Built content for internal board meetings.
  • Helped organize the firm's annual accessibility technology conference.

Venturous

Venture Fellow (part-time) · Aug 2023–Mar 2024

Scouted and diligenced startups and matched them with thesis-aligned venture funds in the Venturous network.

Breinfuel

Consumer startup (nootropic energy drink) · Marketing & BD Representative · 2020–2022

Hired during my sophomore and junior years to scale Breinfuel's CVS retail pilot through campus community engagement.

  • Built the ambassador program: co-developed it and scaled it to 15 Northeast campuses.
  • Drove the retail pilot: activated ambassadors at key New England stores.
  • Result: scaled the CVS pilot from 8 locations to 3,000 nationwide by driving sell-out sales near college campuses.
