01 · Life Sciences Strategy Consulting
Hayden Consulting Group · Consultant · 2024–present

I help biopharma leaders decide what to invest in, how to launch it, and where to take it next.

At Hayden, I've worked on 20+ strategic projects for mid-size biotech to large biopharma companies, covering acquisition diligence, launch strategy, forecasting and US/global expansion. Most of my work is in rare disease and CNS, where technical biology, small patient populations, complex reimbursement and high-stakes regulatory paths make the commercial case hardest to build.
